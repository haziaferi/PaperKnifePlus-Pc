# Blank scanned pages and photo page sizes (2026-11-27)

Two defects found by the purpose map's walk of everyday jobs (`docs/purpose-map.md`), and what was measured to fix them.

## Remove blank pages and scans

**Defect.** `core.pdftools.is_blank` refused every page that holds an image. A scanned page is an image, so the back of a scanned sheet, the common case for this tool, was never found.

**Rule now.** A page that holds images, and has no text, comment, form field or visible drawing, is rendered in grey at 72 DPI with 2 % of each edge left out. Its paper tone is the median grey. The page is blank when that tone is at least 160 and at most 4 pixels differ from it by more than 40 grey levels, darker or lighter. The constants are `_SCAN_*` in `paperknife/core/pdftools.py`.

**Simulated scans.** 68 pages were generated by `_qt-2026-10-09/_blank-scans-2026-11-27/probe.py`: 17 cases, each at 300 and 200 DPI, in colour and grey, as JPEG at quality 75 filling an A4 page. Counts are pixels standing out at 72 DPI and 40 levels.

| Case | Kind | Count | Found blank |
|---|---|---|---|
| empty white paper, grain | blank | 0 | yes |
| cream paper | blank | 0 | yes |
| grey recycled paper (tone 205, heavier grain) | blank | 0 | yes |
| the back page's text showing through (grey 228) | blank | 0 | yes |
| a 1 % edge shadow | blank | 0 | yes |
| eight dust specks, 1-3 px | blank | 8-22 | no: kept |
| two punch holes | either | 500-517 | no: kept |
| a uniform pale yellow sheet | either | 0 | yes |
| one line of 11 pt text | content | 1,337-1,431 | no |
| a page number alone (10 pt) | content | 17-20 | no |
| a handwritten signature | content | 271-286 | no |
| a pencil note (grey 165) | content | 55-77 | no |
| a faded line (grey 150) | content | 819-890 | no |
| a single 9 pt "x" | content | 14 | no |
| "Intentionally left blank" | content | 336-337 | no |
| a full page of text | content | about 47,000 | no |
| a dark picture (tone 21) | content | about 27,000 | no (also darker than paper) |

The smallest content counts 14 and every empty sheet counts 0, so a limit of 4 leaves a margin of more than 3 times below the smallest mark. Dust can count as much as a small mark, so a dusty page is kept: a blank page left in is harmless, a page with a mark removed is not. The question names the pages before anything is removed, and the change can be undone.

**Render resolution.** 72 DPI took 13 ms a page warm. 50 DPI lost the pencil note (count 0 at 50 grey levels), and 100 DPI took 68 ms a page with no better margin. The look runs before the question, on the window's thread, so a 300-page scan with no text pauses about 4 s.

**Real PDFs.** `real_check.py` ran on the 42 PDFs installed programs ship (`C:\Program Files*`): 1,375 pages, 96 of them with images. The old rule and the new rule call the same 6 pages blank, none with images. No page with an image is called blank.

**Not covered.**
- Shadows wider than 2 % of an edge.
- Uneven light across a page photographed with a phone.
- Paper darker than tone 160.

All three keep the page, which is the safe side.

## Photo page sizes in Other → PDF

**Defect.** Each image became a page of one point per pixel: a 4000 × 3000 phone photo made a page about 141 cm wide.

**Rule now** (`core.convert.image_page_size`):
- A resolution of 100 DPI or more, as a scanner writes, sizes the page by it, so the page comes out the size of the sheet.
- A lower resolution, or none, counts as 72 DPI. That covers phones, which write 72, and screenshots, which write 96 or nothing.
- A page larger than A4 (ISO, 595.28 × 841.89 pt) is scaled down to fit A4 in the image's own orientation.
- A smaller image is never enlarged.
- The image keeps every pixel; only the page it sits on changes size.

| Image | Page |
|---|---|
| 4000 × 3000 photo, 72 DPI | 793.7 × 595.3 pt (A4 on its side, height filled) |
| 3000 × 4000 photo, no DPI | 595.3 × 793.7 pt |
| A4 scanned at 300 or 200 DPI | A4 |
| 1920 × 1080 screenshot, 96 DPI | 841.9 × 473.6 pt |
| 300 × 200 image, no DPI | 300 × 200 pt, as before |
| 600 × 300 scan at 150 DPI | 288 × 144 pt, its real size |

HEIC, a common phone format, is still not accepted: reading it needs a new library (`pillow-heif`), which is the owner's call.
