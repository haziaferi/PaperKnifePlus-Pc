# Blank scanned pages and photo page sizes (2026-11-27)

Two defects found by the purpose map's walk of everyday jobs (`docs/purpose-map.md`), and what was measured to fix them.

## Remove blank pages and scans

**Defect.** `core.pdftools.is_blank` refused every page that holds an image. A scanned page is an image, so the back of a scanned sheet, the common case for this tool, was never found.

**Rule now.**
- A page that holds images, and has no text, comment, form field or visible drawing, is rendered in grey at 72 DPI. A very large page is rendered smaller, at no more than 4 million pixels.
- Its paper tone is the median grey of the page away from its edges (2 % of each side left out).
- A pixel more than 40 grey levels from that tone, darker or lighter, is a mark.
- A band 2 % wide along an edge is ignored only when it is marked along at least 95 % of its length. That is a scanner's shadow or a dark border; a footer, a page number or a stamp near the edge has gaps, so it still counts.
- The page is blank when the paper tone is at least 160 and at most 4 pixels are marks.

The constants are `_SCAN_*` in `paperknife/core/pdftools.py`.

**Simulated scans.** 68 pages were generated by `_qt-2026-10-09/_blank-scans-2026-11-27/probe.py`: 17 cases, each at 300 and 200 DPI, in colour and grey, as JPEG at quality 75 filling an A4 page. Counts are pixels standing out at 72 DPI and 40 levels.

| grey recycled paper (tone 205, heavier grain) | blank | 0 | yes |
| the back page's text showing through (grey 228) | blank | 0 | yes |
| a 1 % edge shadow | blank | 0 | yes |
| eight dust specks, 1-3 px | blank | 8-22 | no: kept |

| a page number alone (10 pt) | content | 17-20 | no |

The counts were taken with the first version of the rule, which left the whole 2 % edge band out; the rule now gives every row the same outcome. The smallest content counts 14 and every empty sheet counts 0, so a limit of 4 leaves a margin of more than 3 times below the smallest mark. Dust can count as much as a small mark, so a dusty page is kept: a blank page left in is harmless, a page with a mark removed is not. The question names the pages before anything is removed, and the change can be undone.

**Render resolution and time.**
- 50 DPI lost the pencil note (count 0 at 50 grey levels).
- 100 DPI had no better margin than 72 DPI.
- A scanned page costs 45-55 ms at 72 DPI, measured cold on the simulated scans; an independent review measured up to 87 ms on 300 DPI JPEG pages. Most of it is decoding the scan.
- So the look runs as a job, with progress and Cancel, before the question: a 300-page scan takes 15-25 s without holding the window.

**Real PDFs.** `real_check.py` ran on the 42 PDFs installed programs ship (`C:\Program Files*`): 1,375 pages, 96 of them with images. The old rule and the new rule call the same 6 pages blank, none with images. No page with an image is called blank.

**Not covered.**
- Shadows wider than 2 % of an edge, or along less than 95 % of it.
- Uneven light across a page photographed with a phone.
- Paper darker than tone 160.

All three keep the page, which is the safe side. Two cases go the other way, both in the design:
- A rule line drawn along the whole of an edge, within 2 % of it, reads as a shadow.
- A single full stop alone on a page is under 4 pixels at 72 DPI.

Ink outside the page's crop box is not looked at, as it does not show.

## Independent review (2026-11-27)

A second reader probed the first version of both fixes. It found, and these are now fixed with tests that fail on that version:
- A 14,400 × 14,400 pt page holding an image crashed the look (an image too large for Pillow). It is now rendered at no more than 4 million pixels.
- The look ran on the window's thread, about 20-26 s on a 300-page scan with the window not responding. It is now a job.
- A footer 1.6 % from the bottom edge was cut off with the edge band and the page called blank. The edge band now counts unless it is a shadow strip.
- The image-size rule below had four faults: a stated Letter, Legal or A3 scan was shrunk to A4; a fax's 204 × 98 DPI was dropped and the page squashed; a square photo turned by EXIF kept unswapped axes; and PDF → Other's PNG / JPEG carried no export resolution, so they came back fitted to A4.

It also confirmed no false blank for a 9 pt "7", an 8 pt "1", faded text at grey 190 and 200, a pencil note at grey 175, a yellow word, a rotated page, an image with a soft mask, and empty 1-bit and CMYK pages.

## Photo page sizes in Other → PDF

**Defect.** Each image became a page of one point per pixel: a 4000 × 3000 phone photo made a page about 141 cm wide.

**Rule now** (`core.convert.image_page_size`):
- A stated resolution whose larger axis is 100 DPI or more, as a scanner or a fax writes, sizes the page by it on each axis, so the page comes out the size of the sheet, up to A3 (841.89 × 1190.55 pt).
- A lower resolution, or none, counts as 72 DPI. That covers phones, which write 72, and screenshots, which write 96 or nothing.
- A page larger than A4 (ISO, 595.28 × 841.89 pt) without a stated resolution, or larger than A3 with one (a camera that writes 300 DPI on a 6000 × 4000 photo), is scaled down to fit A4 in the image's own orientation.
- A smaller image is never enlarged.
- The image keeps every pixel; only the page it sits on changes size.
- A photo turned by its EXIF orientation has its two resolutions swapped with it.
- PDF → Other writes its export resolution into each PNG and JPEG, so they come back at their page's size.

| Image | Page |
|---|---|
| 4000 × 3000 photo, 72 DPI | 793.7 × 595.3 pt (A4 on its side, height filled) |
| 3000 × 4000 photo, no DPI | 595.3 × 793.7 pt |
| A4 scanned at 300 or 200 DPI | A4 |
| US Letter, Legal or A3 scanned at 300 DPI | Letter, Legal or A3 |
| 6000 × 4000 camera photo, 300 DPI | 841.9 × 561.3 pt (A4 on its side) |
| 1728 × 1145 fax, 204 × 98 DPI | 609.9 × 841.2 pt |
| 1920 × 1080 screenshot, 96 DPI | 841.9 × 473.6 pt |
| 300 × 200 image, no DPI | 300 × 200 pt, as before |
| 600 × 300 scan at 150 DPI | 288 × 144 pt, its real size |
| an A5 or Letter page exported by PDF → Other, then put back | A5 or Letter |

HEIC, a common phone format, is still not accepted: reading it needs a new library (`pillow-heif`), which is the owner's call.
