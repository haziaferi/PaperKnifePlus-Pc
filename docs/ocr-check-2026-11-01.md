probes) stayed in the work folder beside the repo; this file is their record.

Orientation detection costs about 1 s a page (osd_probe.py, osd_size_probe.py): at
three-quarter size it saved 0.14 s, at half size its answers were wrong (confidence
0.5 and 1.8, which the floor refuses). Its `rotate` is the clockwise turn that makes
the page upright (a page turned 90 clockwise gives 270, confidence 12-14).

Not measured here: real scans (no truth), languages other than English, the
confidence floor 40 against Tesseract's text on noisy pages (ScanKnife+'s question 4
measured it on 31,000 words). The upright pages were already read in full before,
so finding 1 shows no gain on them: its gain is on pages where Tesseract is unsure.

## Finding 4: grey input (2026-11-01)

`grey_check.py` (tables in `grey-check.md`): the three MANUAL pages and a generated
till receipt, each scanned clean, faded (ink 120 on paper 205), on cream paper with
heavy grain, shaded (light falling to half), in blue ink, and the receipt as faded
thermal paper; the rendered page handed to Tesseract as colour (as before), plain grey,
or grey stretched to clip 0.5% at each end (ScanKnife+'s question 8 answer).

| Pages | Colour | Grey | Stretched grey | s/page colour -> grey |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean prose | 1452 | 1452 | 1453 | 3.73 -> 2.95 |
| faded prose | 1451 | 1451 | 1456 | 4.28 -> 3.21 |
| cream prose | 1454 | 1453 | 1454 | 4.00 -> 3.18 |
| shaded prose | 1342 | 1339 | 1339 | 3.88 -> 2.90 |
| blue-ink prose | 1453 | 1452 | 1452 | 4.27 -> 3.38 |
| receipt clean / thermal | 83 / 62 | 83 / 62 | 83 / 62 | 1.26 -> 1.19 / 1.48 -> 1.30 |

Words found of 1458 (prose) and 84 (receipt); orientation right on every page with every
input. The receipt kept 21 of 22 item lines with their price clean, whatever the input;
on the thermal copy the faded prices were lost with every input.

`grey_sparse_check.py` (`grey-sparse-check.md`): pages of one word, one line, three and
eight lines. On a page of one line (ink 0.19% of the page, under the 0.5% the stretch
clips) the stretch read 1 of 12 words clean and 0 of 12 on faded or cream paper; colour
and grey read 12 of 12. Cause (probes): with the clip points inside the paper's grain the
stretch spreads the grain from black to white; on a page without grain they land at
255..255 and PIL leaves it as it is, or at 254..255 and every pixel not pure white turns
black. Seeded grain of +-4 levels is enough: stretch 0 of 11, colour and grey 11 of 11
(the guard test `test_ocr_with_real_tesseract_reads_a_page_of_one_line`).

A shaded sparse page with fresh grain six times over: colour lost the whole 8-line page
twice (0 of 96), grey once read 11 of 36 on the 3-line page; Tesseract is unstable there
whatever the input, so no robustness gain is claimed for grey.

Rendering grey directly in MuPDF (176 ms) is slower than the colour render plus PIL's
conversion (96 ms), whose output is what was measured; the app converts.

Decision: plain grey, not stretched. Equal words, 0.7-1.1 s a page sooner, which wins
back most of the orientation check's second.
